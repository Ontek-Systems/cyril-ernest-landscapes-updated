About This Service

Garden Rooms & Pergolas

A garden room gives you a truly versatile extra space — a home office, studio, gym, games room or simply somewhere to sit and enjoy the garden in all weathers. We design and build bespoke garden rooms that are fully insulated, weatherproofed and finished to a high standard.

Our pergolas and outdoor structures are designed to add architectural character to your garden. Whether freestanding or attached to the house, they create defined outdoor rooms ideal for entertaining, climbing plants or seasonal shade.

Every structure we build starts with a proper foundation and is built to last. We handle all groundworks, structural carpentry and finishing — all owner-led with no subcontractors involved.

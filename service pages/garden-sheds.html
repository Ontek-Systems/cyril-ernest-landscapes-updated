Garden Sheds

A well-built garden shed is an essential part of any functional outdoor space. We design and construct bespoke sheds that are built to fit your garden, your storage needs and your aesthetic preferences — not a generic flat-pack solution.

Every shed we build starts with a proper base — whether a concrete pad, timber frame or paving slab foundation — ensuring the structure remains level, dry and stable for the long term. We use quality timber, properly treated against the elements.

From compact storage sheds to larger workshop-style structures, we build to your specification. All work is owner-led and finished to the same standard as our larger landscaping projects.

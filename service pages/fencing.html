About This Service

Fencing

Good fencing gives your garden privacy, security and a finished, polished look. We install a wide range of timber fencing styles — from solid close-board and featherboard panels to post-and-rail, picket and decorative options.

Every fence we install starts with properly set posts — concreted in or driven depending on the ground conditions — ensuring the whole structure stays upright and secure over the years. We pay attention to line, level and finish so the result looks as good as it functions.

We advise on the best option for your situation, taking into account wind exposure, soil conditions, your desired level of privacy and the style of your property. All work is carried out by our own team, not subcontractors.

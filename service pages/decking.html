About This Service

Decking

A well-designed deck extends your living space outdoors. Whether you want somewhere to dine al fresco, a sun terrace or a raised platform that levels out a sloped garden, we design and build decking that is beautiful, safe and built to last.

We work with both pressure-treated hardwood timber and low-maintenance composite decking, depending on your preference and budget. The substructure — joists, posts and frame — is built to handle the load properly, with correct drainage beneath to prevent deterioration.

Every project is designed around your space, taking into account the sun path, views, access points and how the deck will connect with the rest of your garden. The result is a functional, attractive addition to your outdoor space.

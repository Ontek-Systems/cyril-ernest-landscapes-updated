About This Service

Tree Work

Trees are one of the most significant features in any garden. Properly managed, they add beauty, structure and wildlife value. Left unchecked, they can become a hazard, block light or outgrow their space. We carry out all aspects of tree work safely and professionally.

Our tree work services include crown reduction and lifting, selective pruning, deadwood removal, shaping, stump grinding and full tree removal where necessary. We assess each tree individually and recommend only what is genuinely needed.

All tree work is carried out with proper safety procedures and a thorough tidy-up on completion. If you are unsure whether work on a tree requires planning permission, we can advise you on this as part of the consultation.

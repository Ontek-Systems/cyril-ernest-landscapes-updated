About This Service

Garden Design

A beautifully designed garden begins with listening. We take the time to understand how you live, what you love, and how you want to feel when you step outside. From there, we create a design scheme that is entirely personal to you and your property.

Our garden design service covers everything from initial site assessment and conceptual layouts through to material selection, planting plans and the finished build. Every element is considered — levels, drainage, sun paths, privacy and long-term maintenance — so the result is both stunning and practical.

As an owner-led business, you work directly with Cyril throughout the process. No subcontractors, no miscommunication. Just consistent, expert craftsmanship delivered with genuine care.

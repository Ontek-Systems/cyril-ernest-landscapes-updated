About This Service

Patios

A well-built patio transforms how you use your outdoor space. Whether you want somewhere to entertain, relax or simply enjoy your garden more, we design and install patios that look exceptional and stand the test of time.

We work with a wide range of materials — from classic natural sandstone and slate to modern large-format porcelain and traditional block paving. Every patio is installed on a properly excavated, compacted and drained base, ensuring it stays level and firm for years to come.

No two jobs are the same. We take into account your garden's existing levels, drainage patterns and the way you use the space to create a layout that genuinely works for you.
